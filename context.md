The product is AyeDrive. The default scope is maximum and strictest coverage across the complete device coverage contract. Each family must have a behavioral `AyeAI: AyeAM <device> sim` realization and an implementation-independent conformance test. The normal end-to-end runner continues recoverable/non-critical failures while preserving evidence; fail-fast is reserved for explicit release gates. Simulation, target hardware, HIL, independent review, accredited process/laboratory evidence, regulator applicability, and certification are separate evidence levels.
